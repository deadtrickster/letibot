THESIS: the child ceiling is live only inside one daemon process and one turn. Every restart, every idle window, and every job already backgrounded steps outside it, so "a child's mode only narrows" is a property of the spawn path, not of the ceiling.

FINDING 1 — a reopened child has no ceiling at all, and opens at the daemon's default point. `crates/harnessd/src/harness/open.rs:2214-2217` builds `parent_ceiling` as a fresh cell holding the child's *own* `cfg.mode`, and its comment ("the point it opened at, which the open already clamped to the parent's") is false: the child clamp at `open.rs:2875` is `inherited_by(&have, seats, seat)` — the child's SEAT, never the parent. The point itself is read from a live parent only in the spawn (`harness/children.rs:3946-3958`) and handed over at `children.rs:4112-4114`; `follow_parent` has no other caller in `crates/` outside tests. A child the daemon reopens (`sessions.rs:2151-2164`, `sessions.rs:2164` `..self.base.clone()`) takes `cfg.mode` from `base` — the daemon's `--mode` or `UNSEEN_PROJECT`=always-ask — because `open.rs:669-672` reads the session's own row only when `parent_session_id.is_none()`, and `open.rs:695-697` deliberately skips the store for a child. Scenario: child narrowed to `read-only` under a `read-only` parent; daemon restarts; a head resumes the child (`sessionlog/src/server.rs:1196-1210` re-registers it with its stored parent, so `parent_of` is `Some`); the child comes back at `always-ask` with a ceiling cell of `always-ask`, can `/mode` up to it, and the settings row says "inherited from the parent session" (`children.rs:1787-1791`). Start the daemon `--mode writes-allowed` (`scripts/letibot:3062`) and every resumed child opens at `writes-allowed`. Severity: high. Change: when `parent_session_id.is_some()`, resolve the parent's current point (live cell if the registry holds it, else `ModeStore::session(parent)`) before the clamp, and persist that as the reopened child's point; make `narrow_to_parent` fall back to the store when its cell is not live.

FINDING 2 — a narrowing is not pushed, and does not bound work already admitted. `harness.rs:853 narrow_to_parent` runs only from the child's own `run_rounds` (`turns.rs:1826`) and per call batch (`turns.rs:2652`). No daemon pass rings a child's wake and no wake is rung for it. An idle or parked child therefore holds the wider point indefinitely, and a restart inside that window converts Finding 1 from "stale" to "widened". A backgrounded job and an in-flight call keep the perimeter they were admitted under — `adjudicate.rs:3098-3112 set_mode` clears grants and touches no job — so a child narrowed to `read-only` can still have a job writing the checkout whose result lands on its transcript. Severity: medium. Change: fan the narrowing out from `set_mode_consented` over the parent's live children (ring their wake), and mark/either-kill or re-gate backgrounded jobs whose seat the point no longer allows.

FINDING 3 — a child's `/mode` writes the project-wide row. `modes.rs:291` (`st.set(workspace, mode)`) runs from the same `record` the child's queued door calls (`turns.rs:3295-3310`), unlike `narrow_to_parent`, which writes only `set_session` (`harness.rs:846-870`). A subagent that narrows itself to `read-only` pins the project store, so the operator's next session in that workspace opens at `read-only` — a point the child had no standing to set for it. Severity: medium. Change: `record` takes a "root session" flag and skips `set` for a child.

CONCESSIONS: the spawn and mid-session doors are sound — `within_parent` (`harness.rs:813`) is called before any row is written at all three doors, `mode_past_parent` substitutes the consented point (`harness.rs:826-840`), and both R306 tests (`tests/child_mode.rs:66`, `:125`) genuinely fail with the check removed. Findings 2 and 3 need a live or restarted daemon, not an ordinary session.

ANTICIPATING: position 2 will say the ceiling holds because every door refuses by name — true of the doors it names, and irrelevant to a child whose ceiling cell was never the parent's. Position 3 will say this is the four-systems overlap; my Finding 1 is the concrete bill for it: the mode store, the registry entry and the spawn's live point are three sources and the reopen reads none of them.
