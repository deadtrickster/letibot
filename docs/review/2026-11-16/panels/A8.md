**D1 - severity of the fyi reach label (A8-F5).**
- HIGH: GLM, Kimi, sonnet. `push.rs:296-307` hands the bridge's word to every `push_channel` layer as `event`. The plugin contract promises the room's `Reach` (`plugin-sdk/src/push.rs:29-32`). A layer that pushes only `addressed` silences a named fyi, and `Out::decide` honours it.

**D2 - `suffixed` and family mail (A8-F6).**
