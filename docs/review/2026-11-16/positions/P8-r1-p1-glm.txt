THESIS — Position 1: R306's ceiling is a live-cell artefact, not a property of the session. Every guard (`within_parent`, `mode_past_parent`) consults an `Arc<Mutex<Mode>>` that exists only while the parent's spawn thread lives; the store, which survives, is deliberately not consulted for children. Reopen the child and the ceiling is gone — and the child comes back at the daemon's default point, not its parent's.

FINDINGS

1. HIGH — a reopened child has no ceiling and opens at the daemon default. `follow_parent` has exactly one production call site, the spawn (`harnessd/src/harness/children.rs:4114`). Every other open — a head attaching after a restart, or the bridge reopening by id — goes through `Sessions::harness_as`, which builds the config from the daemon base: `harnessd/src/sessions.rs:2156-2165` (`..self.base.clone()`), whose own comment contemplates "a resumed subagent". `resolve_the_point` then skips the session's own row for children (`harnessd/src/harness/open.rs:669-673`, `parent_session_id.is_none()` guard) and the project row (`:695` branch precedes `:700`), leaving `cfg.mode` = the `--mode` flag (`harnessd/src/cli.rs:717`) or `UNSEEN_PROJECT`. The open-time clamp `open.rs:2874-2877` clamps by the child's seat only, never by the parent's point. With `parent_point == None`, `within_parent` (`harnessd/src/harness.rs:813-823`) is vacuous, so all three `/mode` doors (`harness.rs:765`, `:924`, `sessions.rs:4888`) pass `allow-all` too. Scenario: parent narrowed to `read-only` mid-incident; child followed and the narrowing persisted (`harness.rs:872-875`); daemon restarts; the child is reopened — it comes up at `always-ask` (exec Absent→Ask: already wider than the parent) or at a wide `--mode`, with no ceiling at all. Every call runs past the point the operator set. invariants.md:394 concedes this exact gap ("Left: a child reopened after a restart"). Fix: at open of a child with no live cell, clamp `cfg.mode` by the parent's stored `session_mode` row (`no_wider_than`), and refuse wider.

2. MEDIUM — narrowing is lazy, pull-only, and per-batch. The parent's `/mode` writes only its own cell (`harness.rs:794`); nothing pushes. A child re-reads it at turn start (`harness/turns.rs:1826`) and before each call batch (`:2652`). The "TOCTOU recheck" re-reads script bodies only (`tools/src/runtime.rs:2313-2320`) — a mode move never re-judges an admitted call. Scenario: parent drops to `read-only` while a child's `bash` build (admitted at `writes-allowed`) runs; the command writes on for twenty minutes; background jobs, which outlive the turn, are never re-gated at all. Fix: re-check the point at the runtime's last-moment check for exec calls, and have the parent narrow its live children through the registry it already holds.

3. LOW — the child's persisted point is dead data. `narrow_to_parent` writes `set_session` (`harness.rs:872-875`) and `/mode` records it (`turns.rs:3305`), but the only reader is disabled for children (`open.rs:669-673`). The row says `read-only` forever while the session runs wide — a stale fact for anyone who lists it.

CONCESSIONS — The three live doors are genuinely held and tested (`harness/tests/child_mode.rs:66`, `:125`); the spawn clamp (`children.rs:3946-3958`) reads the point live; a mid-flight child is protected by the OwnThread refusal (`sessions.rs:2141-2147`); the default reopen lands narrower, not wider, unless the flag is wide. To position 2: both R306 tests hand the cell in (`child_mode.rs:48-49`), so they cannot fail on the reopen path by construction. To position 3: the fix is not a fifth system — it is making the store, which already holds the parent's row, the ceiling of record at open.
